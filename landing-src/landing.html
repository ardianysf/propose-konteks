People
  1. 1

    Lead the vision. Decide what should exist, and what should not.

  2. 2

    Set the constraints. The budget ceiling, who may touch what, and what done means.

  3. 3

    Say yes. And own the decision.

  4. 4

    Judge the evidence. Read the plan, the test results, and the receipt. Know when they are thin.

  5. 5

    Hold the context. The things nobody wrote down.
