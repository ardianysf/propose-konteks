How billing works

No token theatre.

Every plan is BYOA

Bring the coding agent you already pay for. Claude and Codex sign in with your subscription; any supported provider connects with a key. Provider tokens and money never enter the Story Point total.

Quotes come before runs

Chargeable stages receive an upfront estimate, a best-effort provider-cost estimate where pricing allows, a budget decision, and an approval boundary before execution.

Allowances scale with seats

Builder, Team, and Scale receive monthly Story Points per paid seat. Team and Scale pool them across the workspace.

Receipts explain reality

Completed work reconciles the approved maximum, actual platform usage, provider telemetry, and delivery evidence.

Project Management mode on every plan

PRDs, breakdowns, tickets, and delivery Reports add no new meter. Chargeable work still happens only through quoted, approved Engineering delivery.

The fine print

Pricing notes.

  1. Published IDR prices are fixed plan prices, not live foreign-exchange quotes. We will announce pricing changes before they take effect.
  2. Free includes no recurring Story Point allowance. Its 0.5 SP is a one-time onboarding grant. Top-ups remain available where the account and payment method permit them.
  3. Enterprise platform and committed Story Point minimums are separate USD-denominated commercial floors. Final enterprise agreements may include deployment, support, security, and volume terms.
  4. Agentic readiness workshops, role-based training, guided onboarding, and adoption coaching are available as professional services. Scope and pricing are tailored to the organization and are separate unless included in an order.
  5. Taxes, model-provider charges, cloud infrastructure outside the plan, and optional paid support may be billed separately where applicable.
