Authority, money, evidence. The same for every agent.

Enforced on the server, never by a prompt.

Authority

Enterprise IAM is built in.

  • Least-privilege roles and groups
  • Access scoped to exact Systems
  • Temporary access with a reason and expiry
  • One audit trail across app, API, and MCP

Money

Every piece of work is quoted before it runs.

  • Your yes locks the ceiling
  • Quotes calibrated from delivery history
  • Automation shadows real work first
  • Provider bill shown separately, never marked up

Evidence

A permanent receipt for every delivery.

  • Quoted, settled, provider cost, evidence
  • The builder never judges its own work
  • Budgets stop before the line
  • Real decisions return to a person
